a little note about privacy

Only what you choose to share

This version keeps mood choices and settings in the current browser when possible. It does not watch activity outside this page, use your camera or microphone, or secretly send data to anyone. Activity tracking, account separation, secure shared photo uploads, and private creator notes need a real backend and explicit sharing controls; they are not enabled here.
